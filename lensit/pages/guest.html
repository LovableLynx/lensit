Read the privacy notice

The photographer running this event is responsible for this data. Your selfie is turned into a face fingerprint (biometric data) only to find your photos. It's matched on the photographer's laptop over this local Wi-Fi, never saved and never sent to the internet. Your results are kept in memory for up to 30 minutes. The event's face data is deleted automatically after the event. No cookies or trackers are used.

This is optional. If you'd rather not use it, ask the photographer for your photos instead. To have the event's face data deleted early, ask the photographer.
