The photographer running this event is responsible for this data. Your selfie is turned into a face fingerprint (biometric data) only to find your photos. The event's face data is deleted automatically after the event. No cookies or trackers are used.

This is optional. If you'd rather not use it, ask the photographer for your photos. To have the event's face data deleted early, ask the photographer.
