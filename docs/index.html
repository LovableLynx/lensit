Privacy by design

Faces stay in the room.

Most selfie-search services upload guests' faces to a cloud server. Lens It matches on the photographer's laptop over the venue's own Wi-Fi.

  • Selfies are processed in memory and never written to disk
  • Face index auto-deletes after 1, 3 or 7 days, or instantly on demand
  • Guests agree to a consent note before the camera opens
  • Setup screens can't be opened from guests' phones
  • Your original photos are never modified

Questions

Good to know.

Do guests need to download an app or sign up?

No. They scan the QR code, agree to a short consent note and take a selfie in their phone's browser.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Your laptop creates or joins a local Wi-Fi network and serves everything itself. Guests connect to that network, not the internet.

What happens to guests' selfies?

The selfie is processed in memory to find matches, then discarded. It's never saved to disk or uploaded anywhere. The face index of your event photos is deleted automatically when the event expires.

What laptop do I need?

An ordinary Windows 10/11 laptop (Mac and Linux can run it from source). It's been built and tested on an 8 GB Intel i5 with no GPU.

How accurate is the matching?

It works best with clear, front-facing selfies and reasonably lit photos. We're testing on real events now and will publish the results, including misses.

How do I get paid?

For now, guests see watermarked previews and you share full photos after payment by transfer. Built-in payments are on the roadmap.

Is it free?

Yes. Lens It is open source. There's no subscription and no per-event fee.
